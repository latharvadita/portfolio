Overview

Designed to
reduce friction

TaskMaster is a mobile task management app designed around one core principle: reduce cognitive load. Tasks are organized into clear sections, today's tasks, upcoming tasks, and progress, so users always know exactly where to look and what to do next.

The design applies visual hierarchy throughout, prioritizing today's tasks at the top while placing key actions like search and the "+" button at the bottom for natural thumb reach. Consistency across labels, forms, and flows ensures users build confidence quickly.

The prototype was evaluated using a Think-Aloud Protocol with 6 participants and a follow-up survey, surfacing real usability insights that shaped a second iteration.

Principles

Design thinking
applied

TaskMaster brought together human-computer interaction principles and real user feedback in a tight loop: design, test, learn, iterate. Every decision was grounded in how people actually behave, not how we assumed they would.
