Demo — Drawing and interacting with polygons in real time

Overview

A canvas where
shapes come alive

The Polygon Project turns basic programming into a colorful interactive playground, blending object-oriented design with a dash of creative experimentation. What starts as a few clicks becomes a dynamic geometric canvas full of vibrant, transparent shapes.

Two core classes: Vertex and Polygon, bring order to the creative chaos. Vertices store coordinate points; polygons assemble them into dynamic, eye-catching shapes, each rendered with unique colors and playful transparency layers.

Interaction

Built to
play with

The interface is entirely mouse and keyboard driven. Left-click to place vertices one by one on the canvas. Right-click to close and complete your polygon, and watch it spring to life with a randomly assigned color and transparency.

Left Click
Place a new vertex on the canvas
Right Click
Close the polygon and render it
E
Erase the most recently drawn polygon
C
Clear the entire canvas
W A S D
Move the selected polygon around the canvas
